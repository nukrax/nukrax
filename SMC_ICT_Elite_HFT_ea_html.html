02 — SMC / ICT Concept Stack

Core Concepts Explained

Every concept used in this EA is rooted in how institutional participants actually operate. No indicators, oscillators, or lagging signals. The EA reads market structure, identifies institutional footprints, and executes only when the full sequence is confirmed.

①
W1 / D1 Macro Bias
Weekly and Daily closes establish the macro direction. Price closing above prior week high = bullish bias. Below prior week low = bearish. All lower TF signals must align with this bias.
②
Premium / Discount
The EA calculates the Daily range midpoint (equilibrium). Buys are only taken in Discount (below midpoint). Sells only in Premium (above midpoint). This eliminates entries at the worst possible price locations.
③
Break of Structure (BOS)
A close beyond a prior swing high (bullish BOS) or swing low (bearish BOS) confirms a structural shift. The EA requires BOS agreement from H4 and H1 before allowing any entry in that direction.
④
Liquidity Sweep
A wick beyond a prior M15 high or low that closes back inside. This confirms institutions have consumed retail stop orders — the engineered liquidity raid. This is the most critical trigger in the entry sequence.
⑤
Order Block (OB)
The last opposing candle before a strong impulse move. For buys: the last bearish candle before a bullish displacement. For sells: the last bullish candle before bearish displacement. Entry price must be inside this zone. Mitigated OBs are automatically rejected.
⑥
Fair Value Gap (FVG)
A 3-candle imbalance where the high of candle 3 does not overlap the low of candle 1 (bullish FVG) or vice versa. Represents an area institutions will return to fill. The EA uses FVG as a confluence filter and SL reference point.
⑦
Displacement
A strong momentum candle whose body is at least 1.8× the body of the prior candle. Confirms institutional intent and genuine directional commitment — not just noise. Without displacement, no OB or FVG setup is valid.
🧲

Liquidity Engineering

Institutions require large amounts of resting orders (liquidity) to fill their positions. They push price into retail stop clusters — equal highs/lows, prior session highs/lows — to trigger those stops, then reverse. This EA is calibrated to detect exactly that sequence.

📐

Market Structure First

Structure is the foundation of every trade. The EA never enters against the dominant structural trend. HTF BOS → MTF BOS → LTF trigger. This top-down alignment ensures the EA is always trading with institutional flow, never against it.

⚖️

Equilibrium Pricing

The Premium/Discount filter prevents buying at the top of a range or selling at the bottom. Institutions buy in discount and sell in premium — the EA only enters when price is in the correct pricing zone relative to the daily equilibrium point.

03 — Entry Decision Engine

The Entry Model

Every trade passes through a sequential multi-stage gate system. Each gate must pass before the next is evaluated. Any failed gate terminates the evaluation immediately — keeping execution fast and preventing false entries. The EA evaluates both bullish and bearish setups on every M15 bar close.

01
Killzone Window Verification
EA checks GMT time. Only London KZ (07:00–10:00) or NY KZ (12:00–15:00) are active windows. All other hours: no entries.
The ICT Killzone filter is the first gate. Based on ICT's core teaching that the highest-probability setups in XAUUSD occur during the London Open and New York Open killzones — because this is when institutional desks are actively executing. The EA uses the InpGMTOffset setting to compensate for broker server time differences. If you are in a DST period, adjust the offset accordingly. During all other sessions (Asian, London Mid, NY Close), the EA monitors structure but places no orders.

02
Risk Limit Gate
Checks daily loss count, daily trade count, weekly loss count, daily drawdown %, and weekly drawdown % before any further evaluation.
If any of the following are true, the entire entry logic is immediately aborted: Daily losses ≥ 2, Daily wins ≥ 3, Daily trades ≥ 3, Weekly losses ≥ 4, Daily equity drawdown ≥ 4%, or Weekly equity drawdown ≥ 8%. When the daily drawdown or weekly drawdown limit is hit, the EA sets a HALTED flag and displays it on the dashboard. The halt is automatically cleared at the start of the next trading day.

03
Spread Filter
Current spread must be ≤ 40 points. During news events or low-liquidity periods, XAUUSD spreads widen significantly — all entries are blocked.
High spreads directly erode edge. If the current spread on XAUUSD exceeds InpMaxSpread (default 40 points), the entry evaluation is skipped. This is critical during major economic news events (NFP, CPI, FOMC) when spreads can spike to 100+ points. The spread is checked in real-time on every entry attempt — not cached. During normal XAUUSD market hours, spreads are typically 8–20 points.

04
HTF Macro Bias Evaluation (W1 + D1)
Weekly and Daily closes are evaluated against prior structure. Both must agree for a strong bias signal. Mixed signals produce a neutral state — reducing trade count.
The Weekly BOS is detected when the latest weekly close exceeds the prior week's high (bullish) or breaks below the prior week's low (bearish). The same logic runs on D1. When both Weekly and Daily agree, the EA sets a STRONG BIAS (+1 or -1). When they disagree, the EA uses the single confirming timeframe but reduces score. This bias must be ≥ 0 for bulls and ≤ 0 for bears — a hard directional gate.

05
Confluence Score Calculation
5-point score system: HTF bias, H4 structure, H1 BOS, Liquidity Sweep, Displacement. Minimum 4/5 required to proceed to entry.
Each of the five pillars awards 1 point:

+1 — HTF Bias aligned (W1 + D1 in same direction)
+1 — H4 Bullish/Bearish Structure (recent close beyond H4 swing)
+1 — H1 Break of Structure confirmed
+1 — M15 Liquidity Sweep confirmed (wick + reversal close)
+1 — M15 Displacement candle (body ≥ 1.8× prior candle body)

Score ≥ 4 = entry evaluation continues. Score ≤ 3 = hard rejection. This eliminates all low-conviction setups and ensures every trade taken has genuine institutional confluence behind it.

06
Order Block Location & Mitigation Check
Identifies the last valid bearish candle (for buys) or bullish candle (for sells) before the displacement impulse. Current price must be inside the OB zone.
The EA scans back InpOBLookback bars (default 20) on the M15 chart. A bullish OB is defined as: a bearish candle (close < open) followed by a bullish displacement candle. The OB zone = Low to High of that bearish candle. The EA then checks if any subsequent candle has closed below the OB low — if so, the OB is considered mitigated and is rejected. Only unmitigated OBs are used as entry zones. Current price (ASK for buys, BID for sells) must be within the OB range for the entry to be valid.
↓ Click to expand
07
FVG Confirmation & SL Refinement
Identifies a 3-candle Fair Value Gap of minimum size. When present, the SL is extended to below the FVG low (buys) or above the FVG high (sells) for additional structural protection.
A bullish FVG exists when: Low[i] > High[i+2] with a gap ≥ InpFVGMinPips (default 5 points). This represents an area of price inefficiency institutions will return to. When an FVG is detected alongside the OB, it acts as a secondary entry zone and SL anchor. The SL for the trade is set to the minimum of the OB-derived SL and the FVG-derived SL — providing the deepest structural protection available at that price location.
↓ Click to expand
08
Premium/Discount Zone Validation
Confirms price is in the correct pricing zone. Buys only in Discount (below Daily equilibrium). Sells only in Premium (above Daily equilibrium).
The EA calculates: Equilibrium = (D1 High + D1 Low) / 2. For buy setups, the current ASK must be below this midpoint (in Discount). For sell setups, the BID must be above it (in Premium). This single filter prevents entries at statistically poor pricing locations — the most common mistake retail traders make. When InpUsePDArray = false, this check is bypassed for highly volatile sessions.
↓ Click to expand
09
SL / TP Calculation & Lot Sizing
SL placed below OB low + buffer (buys) or above OB high + buffer (sells). TP at 2.5R from entry. Lot size calculated from capital × risk% ÷ (SL points × pip value).
Stop Loss: OB boundary ± InpSLBuffer points (default 8). If FVG also detected, SL is extended to the more conservative level.

Take Profit: Entry + (SL distance × InpRR). Default RR = 2.5. This gives a minimum theoretical edge of 2.5:1.

Lot Size: Risk Amount ÷ (SL Points × Value Per Point). Risk Amount = Account Capital × InpRiskPercent / 100. All values are normalised to broker lot step and capped at min/max lot. If the resulting lot is 0, the trade is rejected.

10
Order Execution
Market order sent via CTrade.Buy() or CTrade.Sell() with full SL, TP, magic number, and comment. Slippage capped at InpSlippage points. Daily trade counter incremented.
The EA uses MT5's CTrade library for execution. Fill policy is set to ORDER_FILLING_FOK (Fill or Kill) — if the order cannot be filled at the requested price within the slippage tolerance, it is rejected rather than partially filled at an unknown price. Each executed trade increments the daily trade counter and is tagged with the EA's Magic Number (20240419) for isolation across multiple EA instances.

04 — Trade Management & Exit Logic

Exits & Position Management

Every open trade is managed tick-by-tick. The EA runs a two-stage exit model: a partial close at 1R to secure profit, followed by a dynamic trailing stop using live M15 structure to maximise the remaining runner. This mirrors how professional institutional traders manage winning positions.

Stage 1 — Partial Take Profit (runs on every tick)

E-1
1R Partial Close. When floating profit ≥ 1× the initial risk amount (1R), the EA closes 50% of the position at market. This is configurable via InpPartialClosePC.
E-2
Breakeven Move. Immediately after the partial close, the remaining position's SL is moved to entry price (breakeven). The trade becomes risk-free. Controlled by InpMoveToBreakeven.
E-3
One-Time Flag. Partial TP and breakeven move are tracked per-ticket via GlobalVariable. They fire exactly once per trade — no duplicate closes or accidental multiple adjustments.

Stage 2 — Trailing Stop Loss (runs on every tick)

E-4
Trail Activation Threshold. The trailing stop only activates once the trade is InpTrailStart points (default 15pts) in profit. Before this threshold, the SL is not moved beyond the breakeven level.
E-5
Structure-Based Trail. For long positions, the trailing SL is set to the lowest low of the last 5 M15 bars minus InpTrailStep points. For short positions, the highest high of last 5 bars plus InpTrailStep. This follows price structure rather than a fixed pip distance.
E-6
One-Direction Only. The trailing SL can only move in the direction of profit — never widen. A new trailing level is only applied when it is more favourable than the current SL by at least InpTrailStep points.

Final Take Profit — Full Position

E-7
TP Hit. Remaining 50% of position closes automatically when price reaches the pre-set TP level at 2.5R from entry. This is set at order placement and requires no manual intervention.
E-8
SL Hit. If price moves against the trade before 1R is reached, the full position closes at the structural SL. After 1R partial + BE, the remaining position can only close at breakeven or better.
05 — Risk Architecture

Institutional Risk Management

Risk management is the most critical component of any professional trading system. The EA operates five independent risk layers simultaneously — from per-trade lot sizing to weekly account-level drawdown limits. Each layer functions autonomously and can halt the EA independently.

Per-Trade Risk
1%
Default risk per trade. Capital × 1% ÷ (SL × pip value) = lot size. Fully dynamic — automatically recalculates for every trade based on current account balance.
Max Daily Loss Trades
2
After 2 losing trades in a day, all new entries are blocked for the remainder of that session. Resets automatically at the start of the next trading day.
Max Daily Win Trades
3
After 3 winning trades in a day, the EA stops entering new positions. Prevents overtrading during high-performance sessions.
Max Daily Trades
3
Hard cap on total trades per day (win + loss combined). Aligns with the 2–3 trades per day target — quality over quantity.
Max Weekly Losses
4
After 4 losing trades in a calendar week, the EA halts all new entries until Monday. Prevents compounding losses during a bad market week.
Daily DD Halt
4%
If equity falls 4% below the day's starting balance, all entry logic is immediately suspended. The EA resumes automatically the following day if the halt was day-specific.
Weekly DD Halt
8%
A 8% equity drawdown from the week's starting balance triggers a full weekly trading halt. Prevents catastrophic drawdowns during consecutive losing periods.
Spread Gate
40pts
Maximum spread permitted for any new entry. Blocks all entries during news events, weekend gaps, or broker-side liquidity issues that cause temporary spread spikes.

06 — Execution Rules

Full Execution Rulebook

The complete sequential logic the EA runs on every M15 bar close, condensed into the exact decision tree used internally. Each condition is a hard gate — a failure at any point terminates the evaluation and waits for the next bar.

Pre-Entry Gates (every M15 bar close)

G-1
Killzone check. Current GMT hour must fall in London KZ (07–10) or NY KZ (12–15). FAIL = skip entire bar.
G-2
Spread check. Current spread ≤ InpMaxSpread (40 pts default). FAIL = skip entry, re-check next tick.
G-3
Position count. Open positions with this EA's magic number = 0. FAIL = no new entries until position closes.
G-4
Risk limits. Daily loss < 2, Daily wins < 3, Daily trades < 3, Weekly loss < 4, Daily DD < 4%, Weekly DD < 8%. Any breach = HALT.

Bullish Entry Sequence

B-1
HTF Bias ≥ 0. W1 and/or D1 confirms bullish macro context. Price closed above prior period high.
B-2
H4 Bullish Structure. H4 close above 3-bar prior swing high confirms intermediate bullish structure.
B-3
H1 BOS confirmed. H1 close beyond the highest high in the InpStructureLookback window confirms entry-level bullish BOS.
B-4
M15 Liquidity Sweep. Last M15 bar wicked below prior bar low and closed back above it — sell-side liquidity consumed.
B-5
Confluence score ≥ 4. Sum of above signals. Minimum 4 of 5 pillars required. Score ≤ 3 = hard rejection.
B-6
OB identified. Last bearish candle before a bullish displacement (body × 1.8 multiplier) found within InpOBLookback bars.
B-7
OB unmitigated. No subsequent close below OB low since OB formation. Mitigated OBs are auto-rejected.
B-8
Price inside OB. Current ASK price must be between OB low and OB high. Price outside zone = no entry.
B-9
Discount zone. ASK below Daily equilibrium (D1 High + D1 Low) / 2. Price in Premium = rejected.
B-10
Execute BUY. SL = OB Low − SLBuffer. TP = Entry + (SL distance × 2.5). Lot = Capital × Risk% ÷ SL value. Market buy sent.

Bearish Entry Sequence

S-1
HTF Bias ≤ 0. W1 and/or D1 confirms bearish macro context. Price closed below prior period low.
S-2
H4 Bearish Structure. H4 close below 3-bar prior swing low confirms intermediate bearish structure.
S-3
H1 BOS confirmed. H1 close below the lowest low in the InpStructureLookback window confirms entry-level bearish BOS.
S-4
M15 Liquidity Sweep. Last M15 bar wicked above prior bar high and closed back below it — buy-side liquidity consumed.
S-5
Confluence score ≥ 4. Minimum 4 of 5 bearish pillars confirmed. ≤ 3 = rejected.
S-6
OB identified. Last bullish candle before a bearish displacement found within InpOBLookback bars. OB = that candle's range.
S-7
OB unmitigated. No subsequent close above OB high since formation. Auto-rejected if mitigated.
S-8
Price inside OB. Current BID must be between OB low and OB high. Outside zone = no entry.
S-9
Premium zone. BID above Daily equilibrium. Price in Discount = rejected.
S-10
Execute SELL. SL = OB High + SLBuffer. TP = Entry − (SL distance × 2.5). Lot calculated. Market sell sent.

Trade Management (every tick while position is open)

M-1
Partial TP check. Floating profit ≥ 1R (initial risk amount). If yes: close 50% at market. Move SL to breakeven. Set GlobalVariable flag to prevent repeat.
M-2
Trailing SL activation. Profit ≥ InpTrailStart points. Identify 5-bar swing low (buys) or swing high (sells) on M15. New SL = swing level ± TrailStep.
M-3
Trail direction lock. New trail SL only applied if it improves on current SL by ≥ TrailStep points. SL can never widen.
M-4
SL or TP hit. Broker closes position automatically. OnTradeTransaction fires, updates daily win/loss counter. GlobalVariable for partial TP flag is retained for audit.
